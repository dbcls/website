We are pleased to announce the release of [TogoVar](https://togovar.org/) 2026.1, an integrated database of human genomic variants.<br />
This release expands variant frequency data by incorporating structural variants (SVs) from [JoGo](https://jogo.csml.org/) and [JSV1](https://jmorp.megabank.tohoku.ac.jp/docs/guide-en/dataset_detail/tommo-jsv1/), updating ToMMo to [61KJPN](https://jmorp.megabank.tohoku.ac.jp/docs/guide-en/dataset_detail/tommo-61kjpn_snvindel/), and adding whole-genome sequencing data from approximately 3,000 [BioBank Japan](https://biobankjp.org/en/#gsc.tab=0) participants.<br />
We have also enhanced variant search capabilities with [CADD](https://cadd.bihealth.org/) deleteriousness scores and splicing predictions from [SSCV DB](https://sscvdb.io/), along with user interface improvements such as sharing search conditions via URLs.<br />
For more details, please refer to the [TogoVar release history](https://grch38.togovar.org/doc/history).<br />
