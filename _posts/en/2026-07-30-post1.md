A research article by Dr. Yasunori Yamamoto of DBCLS and his co-authors titled "TogoMCP: natural language querying of life-science knowledge graphs via schema-guided LLMs and the Model Context Protocol" has been published.<br />
The article is open access and is available via the DOI link below.<br />

Kinjo, A. R., Yamamoto, Y., Bustamante-Larriet, S., Labra-Gayo, J.-E., & Fujisawa, T. (2026). TogoMCP: Natural Language Querying of Life-Science Knowledge Graphs via Schema-Guided LLMs and the Model Context Protocol. Database 2026:baag042. https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baag042

TogoMCP uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to connect external large language models with knowledge bases, including the RDF portal, and facilitates effective knowledge acquisition.<br />
In an evaluation covering 23 knowledge bases, TogoMCP showed a significant improvement in question-answering performance.<br />
This achievement is expected to serve as a foundation for the efficient utilisation of life science data without requiring expertise in specialized query languages.<br />
