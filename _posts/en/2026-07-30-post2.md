On Tuesday, 21 July 2026, we launched the redesigned NBDC Human Database portal site.<br />
On the new portal site, we have overhauled the page structure and design to make it easier to access the information required for data submission and use. We have also enhanced the search functions for research information and dataset details, making it easier to search for, verify and apply for the data you wish to use.<br />
The new portal site can be accessed via the same URL as before [https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en](https://humandbs.dbcls.jp/en).<br />
<br />
For applications to submit or use data, as well as for various enquiries, please refer to the information provided on the new portal site.
We will continue to promote the appropriate sharing and utilisation of human data, whilst striving to develop a portal site that is even clearer and easier to use.<br />
